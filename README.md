A warm, funny, heartfelt family scene in two lines, written for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene" bounty ($2 in USDC). Grandma teaches her dumpling recipe over the phone, one fold at a time — and the lopsided ones turn out to look like family.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the scene (151 characters, two lines) to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
